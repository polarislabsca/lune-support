Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Lune is made by Polaris Labs, in Canada. In short: we don't collect any data from the app. What you log stays on your device, and in your own iCloud account if you choose. We run no servers, and Lune contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

What Lune stores, and where

Everything you log (periods, spotting, sex, ovulation tests, temperatures, cervical mucus, pregnancy tests) and your settings are stored only on your device, with the strongest file protection iOS offers. Lune's data is excluded from iCloud Backup. Lune never asks for your name, birth date, email or an account.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud sync, Lune keeps your data the same on your devices through your own iCloud account. The data is end-to-end encrypted: only your devices can read it, not Polaris Labs and not Apple. Apple provides iCloud under its own privacy policy. Turning sync off keeps the data on your device.

Apple Health (optional)

If you turn it on, for the types of data you choose, Lune reads your cycle data from Apple Health and saves what you log in Lune to Apple Health. That data stays in Apple Health on your devices; Lune doesn't send it anywhere else. What Lune saves to Apple Health is outside Lune's app lock, and other apps you allow can read it.

Buy us a coffee

The optional "Buy us a coffee" purchases are made through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details. Lune keeps no record of your purchases, and a purchase unlocks nothing. Apple gives us sales reports that don't identify you.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code in the app.
  • No data sold or shared, because we have none.
  • The app connects to the internet only for iCloud sync (if you turn it on) and for App Store purchases.

If you chose "Share with App Developers" in your device's settings, Apple may send us crash reports and usage statistics that don't identify you. You can change this in the Settings app under Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Files you export

A CSV file or doctor report is created only when you ask for one, and you decide where it goes. These files aren't encrypted: anyone who gets them can read them. Lune deletes its temporary copy when you close the share sheet.

This website and the feedback form

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies or analytics, but GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you visit (see GitHub's privacy statement). The feedback form is provided by Tally (Tally BV, Belgium). When you open the feedback page, your browser connects to Tally, and what you type is sent to Tally and to us. An email address is optional and only needed if you'd like a reply. Please don't include health details. We use feedback only to answer you and to improve Lune, and we delete it on request. Tally's privacy policy applies to the form.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings → Erase data deletes your data on this device, or everywhere (including your Lune data in iCloud and what Lune saved to Apple Health). To have your feedback deleted, contact us.

Children

Lune doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. We will never start collecting your cycle data.
