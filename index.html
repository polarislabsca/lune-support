Lune – Private Period Tracker

Lune is a free period tracker for iPhone and iPad. Your data stays on your device, and in your own iCloud account if you turn on sync. It never reaches us.

Questions

Where is my data?

On your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, it's also in your own iCloud account, end-to-end encrypted so that only your devices can read it. Polaris Labs runs no servers and never receives your data. See the Privacy Policy.

How does Lune predict my next period?

Lune uses the median of your recent cycles and shows a likely range, not a single day, so a few very long cycles don't skew the estimate. In the app, open Settings → How predictions work.

Can I use Lune to avoid pregnancy?

No. Lune is not a method of contraception. Pregnancy is possible on any day of your cycle, and no day in Lune is a safe day. To prevent pregnancy, use a reliable method of contraception every time.

How do I keep a backup?

Turn on iCloud sync in Settings, or export a CSV file in Settings → Import & export and keep it somewhere you trust. We can't recover your data for you: we never have a copy.

How do I move my history from another app?

Settings → Import & export → Import a CSV file. You choose the date and flow columns and see what will be added before anything is saved. You can also import from Apple Health in Settings → Apple Health.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Erase data. "This device" deletes everything Lune stores on this device; "Everywhere" also deletes your Lune data in iCloud and what Lune saved to Apple Health.

Is Lune really free?

Yes. No ads, no tracking, no subscriptions, and nothing is locked. If you'd like to support Lune, you can buy us a coffee in Settings. It doesn't unlock anything.
